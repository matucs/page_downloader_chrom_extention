Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

What we do not collect

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or location
  • Your browsing history or the sites you visit
  • The contents of files you download, or a record of what you downloaded
  • Behavioural or usage analytics
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Login credentials or passwords

What the extension accesses

  • The current tab's content — read only when you click "Scan Page", in order to find downloadable files on it.
  • The download API — used to save the files you select to your computer.
  • Local storage — holds your settings, in-progress download state, and license record. It stays in your browser.

Nothing runs and no page is read until you click "Scan Page".

Third-party services

The extension uses no analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

The single third party it ever contacts is Gumroad, and only if you buy a Pro license. When you activate a license key — and periodically afterwards, to confirm the subscription is still active — the extension sends that key to Gumroad's license verification API at https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify.

Nothing else is sent: no email address, no browsing data, and no list of what you downloaded. Free and trial users never trigger this request. Purchases made on Gumroad's own checkout page are covered by Gumroad's privacy policy.

Data security

Scanning, filtering, and file naming all happen locally on your device. The only network requests the extension makes are the downloads themselves, which go directly to the original file URLs, and the Gumroad license check described above.

Permissions

  • downloads — saves the files you select to your computer.
  • activeTab — grants temporary access to the single tab you are on, and only at the moment you click "Scan Page". The extension has no standing access to any website.
  • scripting — runs the scanning function in the page when you click "Scan Page".
  • storage — keeps your settings and license record in the browser.
